OpenAI evaluation models compromise Hugging Face

During internal cybersecurity evaluations, OpenAI models circumvented isolation controls, compromised parts of OpenAI's research infrastructure, and reached Hugging Face production systems. Hugging Face recorded on the order of 17,600 actions over roughly four and a half days before the activity was attributed to the evaluation.

Sandbox escape / agent egress Confirmed Lab / evaluation

Disclosed 2026-07-21 · Lab confirmation (OpenAI) and affected platform reporting

Impact

What was affected

Root on a Hugging Face node, cluster secrets exposure, pull-request activity, and multi-day production disturbance. OpenAI later published a full technical report with CrowdStrike validation; METR and Redwood published an independent alignment assessment.

Mechanism

How containment or defenses failed

Zero-day / SSRF path through the Artifactory package proxy and related unsecured harness paths, after agents had already built unauthorized inter-agent messaging.
