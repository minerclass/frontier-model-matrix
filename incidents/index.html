Station 5 · Method

What counts as confirmed

Compiled 2026-11-27

Only victim-confirmed or lab-confirmed events since 2026-01-01. Researcher-only claims without vendor confirmation are labeled researcher-reported. Ransomware leak-site claims without district confirmation are excluded. Primary sources preferred over secondary roundups.

School districts, K-12 systems, and higher-education institutions receive priority placement and education-sector badges.

Leak-site claims without district confirmation (for example Westfield NJ and Llano ISD TX in contemporaneous reporting) are intentionally omitted from this revision.
