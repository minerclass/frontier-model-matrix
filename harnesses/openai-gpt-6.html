OpenAI GPT-6 and GPT-6.1 family

OpenAI's published scores across GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, GPT-6.1 Sol (29 Sep 2026), and GPT-6.1 Terra (26 Oct 2026). ChatGPT's GPT-6 control is a thinking slider over Sol and Astra, while GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-6.1 Terra are Work, Codex, and API models.

Primary URL: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/ ↗ · 8 benchmarks · 65 recorded cells

How this harness compiles a score

  • Astra scores come from the Astra launch page and from the Vega-Lite chart payloads embedded in the 26 Oct 2026 GPT-6.1 Terra launch page.
  • Sol and Luna scores come from the 22 Sep 2026 Sol and Luna post and the GPT-6.1 Terra comparison charts.
  • GPT-6.1 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol multi-effort scores come directly from the Vega-Lite chart specs on https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-terra.
  • GPT-6.1 Sol's DeepSWE cell is derived: OpenAI printed a 6.4 point gain over GPT-6 Sol's best (68.8%) and did not print the absolute.

Scope & boundaries

  • None of GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, or GPT-6 Luna has a CursorBench row in the 12 Nov 2026 read.
  • OSWorld 2.1 on the GPT-6.1 Terra page is kept separate from OSWorld 2.0 on the earlier Astra and Sol pages.
  • A derived cell is not a number OpenAI printed.
