OpenAI GPT-6 family

OpenAI's published scores for GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6 Luna, and the GPT-6.1 Sol claim. ChatGPT's GPT-6 control is a thinking slider over Sol and Astra, not a separate scored model.

https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/

How this harness compiles a score

  • Astra scores come from the Astra launch page. The page says scores are the maximum at any effort unless a table names an effort.
  • Sol and Luna scores come from the 22 Sep 2026 Sol and Luna post, at the effort named in each row.
  • GPT-6.1 Sol's DeepSWE cell is derived. OpenAI printed a 6.4 point gain over GPT-6 Sol's best and did not print the absolute.
  • Competitor cells on these pages are quotes from other labs when OpenAI says so.

What this page does not claim

  • None of GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol, or GPT-6 Luna has a CursorBench row in the 13 Oct 2026 read.
  • A derived cell is not a number OpenAI printed.
