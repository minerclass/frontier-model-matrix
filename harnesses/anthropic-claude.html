Anthropic Claude 5.5 and Fable 5.2 posts

Headline scores from Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 post (22 Sep 2026), Claude Sonnet 5.5 post (28 Sep 2026), Claude Haiku 5.5 post (7 Oct 2026), and Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 post (9 Nov 2026). Each row carries the sourceUrl and sourceDate of the post that printed it.

Primary URL: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5 ↗ · 19 benchmarks · 120 recorded cells

How this harness compiles a score

  • Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Haiku 5.5, and Fable 5.2 cells use each model's highest-available effort with safeguards on unless the post names another effort or notes Mythos / without-safeguards runs.
  • Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 share the same underlying model; Fable 5.2 carries cyber and biology safeguards for general availability ($8 input / $40 output per million tokens), while Mythos 5.2 is restricted to Anthropic's Verification Programs.
  • Haiku 5.5 ($0.10 / $0.50 up to 100k tokens) is Anthropic's first Haiku-class model with an adjustable effort setting.
  • AutomationBench, AA-Briefcase, GDPval-AA, and CursorBench rows quoted by Anthropic are marked quoted or unspecified according to how Anthropic attributed them.

Scope & boundaries

  • On CyScenarioBench, Anthropic notes that each lab's scores are based on unique tasks, so CyScenarioBench rows are not common-task comparisons.
  • GDPval-AA and AA-Briefcase Elo scores are kept separate from percent rubric scores.
