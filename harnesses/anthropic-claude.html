Anthropic Claude 5.5 posts

Headline scores from Anthropic's Opus 5.5 post on 22 Sep 2026 and the Sonnet 5.5 post. CursorBench cells match Cursor's board. Several GPT cells are quoted from OpenAI.

https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5

How this harness compiles a score

  • Opus 5.5 results use adaptive thinking at max effort unless the post names another effort.
  • Terminal-Bench for Opus 5.5 is xhigh. Astra's Terminal-Bench cell is quoted from OpenAI.
  • Sonnet 5.5 cells come from the Sonnet post. FrontierCode for Sonnet is the extra-high figure, 52.1%. Max is 46.2% and is not the cell stored here.
  • AutomationBench was run by Zapier. Anthropic says safeguard interventions counted as failures.

What this page does not claim

  • Sonnet has no AutomationBench or HLE cell on the Sonnet table, so those rows are omitted.
  • GDPval-AA is Elo and is stored separately from percent scores.
