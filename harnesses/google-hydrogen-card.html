Gemini 4 Hydrogen model card

The 10-benchmark comparison table on the Gemini 4 Hydrogen model card (12 Nov 2026) against Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Terra, plus the AA Intelligence Index v5.0 scores reported in Google's launch post.

https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-hydrogen/

How this harness compiles a score

  • Google's methodology page (deepmind.google/models/evals-methodology/gemini-4-hydrogen) documents which rows Google self-computed and which came from Artificial Analysis, Andon Labs, or official public leaderboards.
  • Hydrogen offers four thinking levels: low, medium, high (default), and xhigh. Card results use the highest thinking setting unless noted; Terminal-Bench 4.1 uses high effort.
  • Flat API pricing across the 1M context window is $1.50 input and $7.50 output per million tokens.

What this page does not claim

  • Terminal-Bench 4.1 (cheating-adjusted) has no GPT-6.1 Terra score on the card, so that cell is omitted.
  • Google self-computed Sonnet 5.5 on Agents' Last Exam v1.1 and OSWorld-2.1, and GPT-6.1 Terra on Chartography; those competitor rows stay rival on the hub.
