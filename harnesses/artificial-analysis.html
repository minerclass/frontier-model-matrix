Artificial Analysis

Artificial Analysis independent evaluations across the 12 Nov 2026 LLM Leaderboard (Intelligence Index v5.0), the 26 Oct–10 Nov 2026 model and benchmark articles (GPT-6.1 Terra, Index v5.0, Gemini 4 Carbon, Muse Ember, and MKA), and the preserved 12 Oct 2026 Muse Wonder Index v4.3 article.

https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models

How this harness compiles a score

  • Intelligence Index v5.0 (launched 27 Oct 2026) combines nine evaluations across knowledge work, coding, science, and general reasoning, with 60% of the index on private or held-out test sets.
  • Index v4.3 scores from the 12 Oct 2026 Muse Wonder article are kept on separate benchmark ids so they are never mixed with v5.0.
  • Coding Agents Index scores run each model inside a real coding agent product (Claude Code, Antigravity CLI, Muse Code, or Codex).
  • MKA (Multimodal Knowledge Agent, launched 10 Nov 2026 with Google DeepMind) runs 200 private visual knowledge-work tasks in AA's Stirrup harness and grades Climb WSR.

What this page does not claim

  • On the 12 Nov 2026 live v5.0 leaderboard, Gemini 4 Carbon (high) is 60 and Claude Opus 5.5 (max) is 59, one point above the 59 and 58 printed at Index v5 launch on 27–28 Oct 2026 as AA added models and recalibrated.
  • Muse Wonder Full has no per-token API price, so AA does not publish a cost per task for it.
