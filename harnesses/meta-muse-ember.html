Meta Muse Ember and Muse Spark Open

Meta's 9 Nov 2026 Muse Ember evaluation table (compared against Claude Haiku 5.5, GPT-6 Luna, and Muse Spark 1.3 at max effort) and Meta's 15 Oct 2026 Muse Spark Open model card.

https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember

How this harness compiles a score

  • Muse Ember ($0.10 input / $0.50 output per million tokens up to 200K context, trained using Muse Wonder Full) is evaluated at max reasoning effort.
  • Meta's evaluation report (research.meta.ai/static/muse-ember-evaluation-report) identifies which Muse Ember rows come from Artificial Analysis (Index v5.0, AutomationBench-AA, Terminal-Bench 4.0, Coding Agent Index-AA, FrontierCode 2.0-AA, GDP.pdf-AA) and which come from Meta's internal common-loop suite (Agents' Last Exam Linux subset, PSI, OSWorld 2.1, PerceptionBench).
  • Muse Spark Open (816B total / 42B active open-weight MoE under the Muse Model License, 15 Oct 2026) is evaluated at xhigh reasoning effort on its Hugging Face model card.

What this page does not claim

  • In Meta's Muse Ember blog post, the AutomationBench-AA chart prints 37.8% for Ember, 20.2% for Luna, and 13.9% for Haiku 5.5, whereas Artificial Analysis's same-day Muse Ember article reports 68.1% for Ember under AA's updated v5 AutomationBench-AA scoring.
  • Muse Spark Open's comparison table compares it against open-weight models and Mistral Large 4 rather than proprietary frontier flagships.
