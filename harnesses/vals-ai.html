Vals AI leaderboards

Vals AI's GDP-weighted Vals Index v2.2 (released 19 Oct 2026, read 12 Nov 2026) and its component leaderboards across Finance Agent v2.1, Excel Modeling Benchmark v1.1, Terminal-Bench 4.0 (mini-swe-agent), Legal Research Bench, HLAB-Vals-1.1, and Tax Agent Bench.

Primary URL: https://www.vals.ai/benchmarks/vals_index ↗ · 8 benchmarks · 169 recorded cells

How this harness compiles a score

  • Vals AI runs every model in shared default harnesses and scores against private test splits.
  • Vals Index v2.2 combines Finance (8.0 GDP weight), Coding (5.6), Legal (1.2), and Tax (0.5), divided by 15.3.
  • Vals AI's Terminal-Bench 4.0 leaderboard runs every model in mini-swe-agent (avg@3 over 3 runs, 8-hour limit) and is kept on its own benchmark id so it is not mixed with provider agent-product claims or AA's runs.
  • Finance Agent v2.1 is kept separate from Finance Agent v2 (on the earlier Argon and Wonder tables) because Vals changed the jury, hedging rules, and rubrics.

Scope & boundaries

  • Vals evaluates Claude Fable 5.2 at High effort, Gemini 4 Carbon and Argon at High effort, Gemini 4 Hydrogen at xhigh effort, and GPT-6.1 Terra, Muse Ember, and Kimi K3.1 Pro at Max effort.
  • HLAB-Vals-1.1 and Tax Agent Bench use strict all-pass rubrics under v2.2, producing lower numbers than partial-credit versions.
