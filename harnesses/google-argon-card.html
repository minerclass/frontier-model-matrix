How this harness compiles a score

  • Argon cells are Google's reported results for its own model.
  • Competitor cells are marked unspecified because the card does not say Google re-ran every one.
  • A blank on the card is omitted here. It is not stored as zero.
  • Methodology link on the card: deepmind.google/models/evals-methodology/gemini-4-argon.

What this page does not claim

  • This table is Google's compilation. It is not CursorBench.
