Gemini 4 Carbon model card

The 17-benchmark evaluation table on the Gemini 4 Carbon model card, published 10 Nov 2026 (reporting October 2026 results). Carbon leads 15 of 17 rows on this card; Astra leads FrontierSWE 2 and Opus 5.5 leads OSWorld-2.1.

Primary URL: https://deepmind.google/models/model-cards/gemini-4-carbon/ ↗ · 17 benchmarks · 80 recorded cells

How this harness compiles a score

  • Google's methodology page (deepmind.google/models/evals-methodology/gemini-4-carbon) states which rows were self-computed by Google and which were sourced from Artificial Analysis, Vals AI, Zapier, Andon Labs, Proximal, Surge, Collinear AI, or rival reports.
  • Carbon and Argon rows self-computed by Google are marked measured; rows Google cites from external leaderboards or rival reports are marked quoted with the named source.
  • GraphWalks 256k-to-1M was self-computed by Google for all five models, so competitor rows stay rival on the atlas hub because Google ran them rather than their makers.
  • Cybersecurity results (CWE-bench v1, ExploitGym, SEC-bench Pro) reflect Carbon when accessed through Google's Fairwind Program for verified defenders.

Scope & boundaries

  • Standard pricing is $4 input and $20 output per million tokens, with a 50% launch discount ($2 / $10) through 30 Nov 2026.
  • Agents' Last Exam v1.1 uses the 17 Oct 2026 122-task release in the ALE-Claw harness and is kept separate from v1.
