CursorBench 4.0

Cursor runs agents on ambiguous multi-file tasks taken from real Cursor sessions. This is the shared coding harness in the repository. Cost, tokens, and steps are Cursor's per-task averages across the 90 configurations on the 12 Nov 2026 board.

Primary URL: https://cursor.com/cursorbench ↗ · 1 benchmarks · 93 recorded cells

How this harness compiles a score

  • Each row is one model at one effort setting.
  • The score is a percent. Higher is better.
  • Cursor names the effort. Composer 2.5 is listed without an effort and stored here as Default.
  • A model absent from this file had no row on the board when it was read on 12 Nov 2026.

Scope & boundaries

  • GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6 Luna, Muse Wonder Full, Muse Ember, Muse Spark Open, and Kimi K3.1 Pro have no row on the 12 Nov 2026 board.
  • Gemini 4 Argon Minimal (48.8%) and both Gemini 3.8 Flash rows appeared on the 13 Oct 2026 board and were not on the 12 Nov 2026 board.
  • These scores are not comparable, one for one, with Terminal-Bench or DeepSWE.
